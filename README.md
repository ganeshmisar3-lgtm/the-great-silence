# Episode 01 — The Vanishing

**Date:** 14 September 2026

At exactly 9:00 AM, every human on Earth vanishes in a single second.

Cars stop. Airports fall silent. Cities become empty.

Only two people remain alive:
- Ganesh
- His female best friend

Standing in the middle of the deserted city, Ganesh whispers:

“Hum... akele reh gaye?”
